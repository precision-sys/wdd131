Overview

Purpose

Giant Sourcing’s website aims to present the company as a reliable partner for corporate support services, helping businesses establish, manage, and grow efficiently. It provides clear information about available services, builds trust through transparency and professionalism, and makes it easy for potential clients to get in touch. The site is designed to attract entrepreneurs, SMEs, and investors seeking practical, affordable, and locally informed business support solutions.

Audience

Giant Sourcing helps business owners, small and medium-sized companies, and organizations get the support they need to grow. Our website is for people starting a new business who need help with company registration, as well as for established businesses looking for bookkeeping, tax, or HR support. We also serve foreign investors and NGOs who want local guidance to run their operations smoothly.
